# Privacy Policy

**Last updated: August 2026**

This privacy policy describes how Delpic ("the app"), developed by Maccara Studio, handles your information.

**Not collecting or sharing your personal data isn't just a technical detail for us — it's a core principle of how Delpic is built.** Your photos and videos are yours; we designed the app so we never see them, store them, or have any way to access them ourselves.

## What Delpic accesses

Delpic asks for access to your device's photo and video library (camera roll) so you can review and organize it. This is the only permission the app requests.

- On Android, you may grant access to your entire library or select specific items ("limited access").
- On iOS, you may grant full access or select specific items.

You can change or revoke this permission at any time from your device's system settings.

## What Delpic does with your data

Everything happens **locally on your device**:

- Photos and videos are only ever read from and deleted through your device's own operating system — Delpic never uploads, transmits, or has any way of accessing your photos or videos over the network.
- Your review progress (which items you've already looked at) and your settings (like autoplay preferences) are stored locally on your device only, using on-device storage. This data never leaves your device.
- Deleting items in Delpic triggers your device's own native deletion confirmation — Delpic does not maintain a copy of anything you delete.

Delpic does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any of your data — photos, videos, or usage data — with Maccara Studio or any third party.

## Third-party services

Delpic does not integrate any third-party analytics, advertising, or data-collection services.

## Children's privacy

Delpic does not collect, store, or transmit personal information from any user, of any age.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the update will be reflected here with a new "Last updated" date.
